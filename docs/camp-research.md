# The 22 real camps — research notes (verified August 2026)

Structured data: `camp-research.json` (ids match `CampCatalog.swift` and
`CampMedia/` folder names). Every camp verified against its official
website; coordinates are camp-property points geocoded from official
street addresses (US Census + OpenStreetMap, cross-checked).

## Identity corrections vs. the video file names

- **"New York Teen Camp" = French Woods Sports & Arts Center** (Hancock,
  NY). No camp uses the file's name publicly, but nyteencamp.com and
  newyorkteencamp.com both 301-redirect to fwsportsarts.com. Confidence:
  medium — confirm with whoever named the file.
- **"Caribou Camp" = Camp Caribou**, Winslow ME (boys, Lerman family,
  est. 1922). Word order reversed from the file name; no other Northeast
  candidate exists.
- **Berkshire Trails Camp** is the rebrand of **Kutsher's Sports Academy
  (KSA)** — keep the old name as an alias. Co-ed, not girls.
- **"Woodward Camp"** currently brands as **Woodward PA** (POWDR-owned).
  Gymnastics is gone from the summer lineup — it's skate/BMX/MTB/scooter/
  parkour now.
- **Camp Poyntelle** retired the "Lewis Village" suffix (survives as the
  grade-8+ division name). Only nonprofit in the list (Commonpoint /
  UJA-Federation NY).

## Camp families / umbrellas

- **Black family (Greeley, PA):** Pine Forest (co-ed, 1931), Lake Owego
  (boys, 1961), Timber Tops (girls, 1963). All within ~3 km.
- **Belgrade Lakes cluster (Oakland/Smithfield, ME):** Camp Manitou
  (boys) + Camp Somerset for Girls (brother/sister affiliation, separate
  owners) + Camp Eastwood (new co-ed starter camp physically on the
  Manitou grounds).

## Religion

- **Jewish (affiliated):** Camp Modin (oldest continuously operating
  Jewish camp in the US), Camp Poyntelle ("Joyfully Jewish").
- **Jewish traditions, no formal affiliation:** Pine Forest, Lake Owego,
  Timber Tops (Shabbat/Motzi kept, not marketed as Jewish camps).
- **Jewish heritage, currently none stated:** Camp Akiba (closed ~1997,
  reopened 2025 under new families with a secular framework).
- **Nondenominational with parallel Shabbat + ecumenical services:**
  Camps Kenwood & Evergreen.
- All others: none stated.

## Coordinate caveats

- **Camp Somerset** is street-level, not rooftop (Somerset Lane centroid
  on East Pond).
- **Raquette Lake Camps** is two properties; the point used is the Girls
  Camp / Antlers Rd side. Boys Camp sits across the lake on Woods Point.
- **Academy Camps** runs two rented campuses: Suffield Academy CT (point
  used) and Blair Academy, Blairstown NJ (40.9829, -74.9620). Swap if
  filming was at the NJ site.
- **Independent Lake Camp**: ±0.5 mi; do not confuse with Camp Westmont
  on the same lake's east shore.

## Film lengths (from the delivered .aivu masters)

Four films run ~95 seconds — Manitou, Eastwood, Berkshire Trails,
Kenwood & Evergreen. The rest run 3–5 minutes. Relevant to booth pacing
and the "2–3 visits then reset" flow.
